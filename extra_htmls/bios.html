Personality

Mira is energetic, curious, and quick to jump into action. She approaches danger with confidence and keeps a positive attitude even when the world is under attack by hostile chickens. Mira is known for her fast reflexes, sharp instincts, and upbeat spirit. She prefers exploring new areas, helping others, and solving problems with determination rather than fear.

Abilities

  • High mobility and fast directional movement
  • Quick reaction time during enemy encounters
  • Skilled with basic ranged weapons
  • Strong adaptability in chaotic environments

Mission

Protect the world from the sudden chicken uprising and restore peace to every region affected by the attack.
