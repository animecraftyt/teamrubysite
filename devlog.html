Amy’s Epic Adventure — Devlog 2

Spoilers ahead. Click “Show It” to reveal the boss-fight details.

For the Eggman / Robotnik Boss Fight

1. Overview & Settings

Total Hits: 6 total to defeat the boss.

Player Resources: 15 rings provided for the encounter.

Character Customization: Appearance and theme music dynamically change based on whether the player’s settings call the boss “Eggman” or “Robotnik”.

Audio for when the boss appears:

  • If Eggman: Sonic Adventure 2 Eggman theme.
  • If Robotnik: Adventures of Sonic the Hedgehog (AoStH) theme.

Boss themes:

  • If Eggman: Sonic Adventure 1 boss theme.
  • If Robotnik: Sonic the Hedgehog (SatAM) Robotnik theme boss remix version.

The boss appearance:

  • If Eggman: classic Eggman.
  • If Robotnik: Robotnik from SatAM.

2. Phase 1 (Hits 1–3)

Inspiration: Classic Green Hill Zone boss fight from Sonic 1.

Mechanic: The Egg Mobile swings a dangerous saw blade instead of the standard ball-and-chain.

3. Phase 2 (Hits 4–6)

Escalation: Track speed increases and the atmosphere intensifies.

New Attacks & Minions:

  • A cannon is mounted on the bottom of the Egg Mobile, shooting projectiles.
  • Classic cartoon minions—Scratch, Grounder, Coconuts, and Snively—join in to drop bombs from above.
