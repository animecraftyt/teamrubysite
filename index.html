Unexpected Honk Has Occurred

Rating: All Ages

Genre: Visual Novel

Description

Unexpected Honk Has Occurred is a short, experimental visual novel created by Team Ruby using Ren’Py. This demo was made in just 3 days, so expect something small, weird, glitchy… and a little chaotic.

You begin the game normally, choosing your name and preparing to play — until a strange “Goose Virus” takes over your screen. Before you can react, an AI named [redacted] appears, trying to stop the corruption before it spreads to your game.

But the Goose Virus doesn’t want to let go.

Your Choice

  • Try to help her delete the Goose Virus
  • Say nothing and let the game close itself

Your decision determines whether the loop continues… or breaks.

Features

  • A short interactive story
  • Glitch effects and corrupted visuals
  • Two endings (Good Ending & Bad Ending)
  • More content and a full story planned for future updates

Thank you for playing, supporting Team Ruby, and being part of the loop. Can you break it?

About Team Ruby

Team Ruby is a small indie game development group creating fun, retro‑inspired projects. Right now, it’s a solo-run studio building games, learning, and experimenting with new ideas. We focus on low‑poly or 2D worlds, with simple or hard gameplay, and creative ideas that feel like classic games with a modern twist.

Our current projects include Chicken Shooter 64, Amy’s Epic Adventure, Paper Sonic the Hedgehog, Unexpected Honk Has Occurred, and Stickman Adventure.

We’re all about experimenting, learning, and making games that are colorful, weird, and full of personality. As the projects grow, the goal is to keep building Team Ruby into something bigger and stronger.

Why I Upgraded the Site

Old Site Version 1 was my very first test. I wanted to see if I could make a website for myself, long before Team Ruby existed. It was simple, but it helped me learn how websites work.

Old Site Version 2 was me trying something bigger. It had achievements, a CRT filter, and more features, but the platform’s limits didn’t feel right for what I wanted. Even with the cool effects, it wasn’t flexible enough.

This new site is the one I’m proud of. It’s cleaner, bigger, and built the way I want it. Hopefully, this will be the last time I remake the Team Ruby website — and the version I stick with as the team grows.
