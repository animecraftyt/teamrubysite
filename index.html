Chicken Shooter 64

Genre: First‑Person Shooter

Rating: All Ages

Content: Fantasy Violence, Comical Shenanigans

Description

Meet Mira Bluetail — the cheerful fox heroine of a world gone to war! When a flock of chickens suddenly arm themselves with knives and declare war on the world, Mira will have her trusty shotgun and leap into the action. In this epic adventure you’ll explore bright, blocky landscapes, dodge wild chicken attacks, and blast through waves of feathery foes in fast, silly, over‑the‑top battles. Mira’s light‑blue dress may look cute, but don’t let it fool you — she’s quick, clever, and ready to take on any poultry uprising that comes her way. Can Mira Bluetail restore peace to her land, or will the chickens rule the world? this is a fight like you’ve never seen before. grab a copy at your local video game store to find out

Unexpected Honk Has Occurred

Genre: Visual Novel

Rating: All Ages

Description

this game is a short, experimental Ren’Py game created by Team Ruby. This demo was made in just 3 days, so expect something small, weird, glitchy… and a little chaotic. You begin the game normally, choosing your name and preparing to play — until a strange “Goose Virus” takes over your screen. Before you can react, an AI named [readacted] appears, trying to stop the corruption before it spreads to your game. But the Goose Virus doesn’t want to let go. As the player, you’ll make a single important choice: Try to help her delete the Goose Virus Say nothing and let the game close itself Your decision determines whether the loop continues… or breaks. This demo includes: A short interactive story Glitch effects and corrupted visuals Two different endings (Good Ending & Bad Ending) This is only the beginning. More content, and a full story will be added in future updates. Thank you for playing, supporting team ruby, and being part of the loop. Can you break it?

About Team Ruby

Team Ruby is a small indie game development group creating fun, retro‑inspired projects. Right now, it’s a solo-run studio building games, learning, and experimenting with new ideas. We focus on low‑poly or 2D worlds, with simple or hard gameplay, and creative ideas that feel like classic games with a modern twist. Our current projects include Chicken Shooter 64, Amy’s Epic Adventure, Unexpected Honk Has Occurred and Stickman Adventure. We’re all about experimenting, learning, and making games that are colorful, weird, and full of personality. As the projects grow, the goal is to keep building Team Ruby into something bigger and stronger.
